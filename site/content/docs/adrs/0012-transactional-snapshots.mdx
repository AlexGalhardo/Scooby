Date: 2026-11-03. State: implemented in the delegated personal scope.

## Context

The previous logical model enumerated several tables. In the single-account MVP, the
fundamental invariant is to persist the reservation and intent atomically and preserve
idempotent fills. Separating each entity into a table would add coordination.

## Decision

`trading_accounts` stores a versioned JSON snapshot per account with orders/intents,
reservations, positions, fills, decisions, policies, heartbeat, cursors and generations.
Every mutation uses a transaction, a PostgreSQL row lock and an expected version. The outbox
is the durable list of orders approved within the snapshot itself; external sending occurs
after the commit. Decimals from shopspring/decimal are serialized as strings,
with no floats for cash, limits or fills. JSON is kept in a portable TEXT column for
the SQLite test database; do not claim normalized NUMERIC where it was not used.

`datasets` and `strategies` store immutable configurations; `backtests` keeps
job/state/summary and `artifacts` preserves the full curve/fills outside polling.
`audit_events` records actions and policy changes before/after in the same
transaction, including risk rejections. Actor is owner/engine/operator.

## Tradeoffs

It simplifies invariants and replay, but the snapshot grows with history and is
rewritten on mutations. This is a personal terminal design, not HFT/SaaS.
Do not truncate the ledger to gain space. Before high volumes, migrate fills,
orders and decisions to normalized tables with reconciliation and balance
comparison. No benchmark was made for millions of events.

## Recovery and ambiguity

A send timeout keeps the intent `unknown`; there is no blind retry. Cumulative fills
are reconciled by the difference in quantity and notional, with deduplication.
The PostgreSQL snapshot does not offer an atomic transaction with the broker.

A new B3 replay requires a block and no exposure/orders; the previous snapshot
is archived in full in `artifacts`, in the same transaction as the reset, with a link
in the audit. No previous fill is deleted.
